        Keep the data moving
        
After the demo is built, the data stands still: nothing closes and nothing new arrives, so Revenue Insights' "last 7 days" changes, Closed Won, the Changes tabs and the New tab go flat. Run these before a demo. Each previews first, and each takes about a minute; CRM Analytics then needs its 45–60 minute refresh.

        
Roll forward plays the days since the last roll through the open deals: closes and slips, stage moves, deals turning at risk, amount and close-date drift, logged activity. Culprit segments slip and lose products more, the counter-trend segment less.

        

            
            
        

        
Add deals creates new expansion deals on existing accounts, for products they don't own yet (the whitespace EAM shows). Counter-trend segments generate more than culprit ones. It fills the New tab and moves pipeline coverage.
